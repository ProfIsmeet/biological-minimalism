# Visualization nonvisual equivalence

Native accessibility trees exposed titles, concise purposes, units, categorical/exact values, missing-data language, scientific boundaries, and tables/lists where visual inference would otherwise be required. Coverage includes integrity rings, HR trend, synchronized signals, coverage radar, pipeline strip, fault/recovery table, research small multiples and matrices, Digital Twin semantic summary, AI Insights, and Mission Timeline.

No percentage confidence was inferred from categorical geometry; unavailable values were not presented as zero; EEG/EOG were explicitly unavailable in S14 rather than fabricated; raw waveform samples were summarized rather than dumped. Exact VoiceOver pronunciation/order remains `PARTIAL` because speech was not captured.
