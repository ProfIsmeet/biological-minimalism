# Furkan public dashboard guide

Open `https://biological-minimalism-iac.vercel.app/mission-overview` in a clean presentation window. Allow up to about two minutes for a sleeping Render Free service to wake; avoid repeated reloads. A ready screen says `PUBLIC · READ-ONLY`, `RECORDED REPLAY`, `CONNECTED`, and `PPG-DaLiA · S14`.

Suggested jury path:

1. Mission Overview: name the three body regions and five-modality architecture.
2. Live Signals: show real recorded S14 PPG, IMU, and ECG and explain that EEG/EOG are unavailable rather than fabricated.
3. Mission Overview: explain PPG + IMU → HR, categorical integrity, and the fault/recovery evidence table.
4. Digital Twin: state “architecture only, untrained, unvalidated”; do not imply a trained personalized twin.
5. Experimental Research: show exact tables and the mixed/negative evidence boundary.
6. Mission Timeline: close on provenance and staged scope.

If the backend is waking, continue with System Brief, Digital Twin, or Experimental Research; those explanatory routes remain useful. Then return to Mission Overview after the connected header appears. If recovery takes longer than roughly two minutes, open Settings, activate Test connection once, and reload Mission Overview once.

Do not claim live astronaut monitoring, clinical use, population validation, live EEG/EOG, confidence percentages, or a trained Digital Twin. The public controls are deliberately unavailable. For ordinary visual presentation, confirm VoiceOver is off unless a screen-reader demonstration is intended.
