## Correction to the master prompt's assumed asset inventory

The task brief assumes a CesiumMan-derived GLB asset (`public/models/holographic-human/cesium-man.glb`) with provenance/license docs and a `HolographicHumanModel.tsx`. None of these exist on this branch or SHA — confirmed by exhaustive search (`find public -type d`, `find . -iname "*PROVENANCE*" -o -iname "*LICENSE*"`). The actual system is **100% procedural geometry**: capsule/sphere meshes built from `humanGeometry.ts`'s `JOINTS`/`BODY_SEGMENTS` tables, rendered by `HumanMannequin.tsx`, plus an older, separate procedural system (`holographicGeometry.ts` / `HolographicHumanFigure.tsx`) used by the operational avatar and the Digital Twin stage. This audit proceeds against the actual repository state, not the assumed one — see `ASSET_AND_LICENSE_AUDIT.md` for the resulting asset decision.

## Conclusion

The existing system's underlying math (geometry, materials, framing, fallback, reduced-motion) is sound and directly reusable. The weaknesses are specifically in the **default visual presentation** of the Digital Twin figure (transparency/silhouette, ambient scan-ring decoration, uncontrolled auto-rotation, clipped framing at rest) — exactly the category of concern Stage 7 is scoped to explore. The prototype targets those specific findings; it does not re-litigate or duplicate the parts already working correctly.
